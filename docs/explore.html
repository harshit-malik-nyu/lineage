What is downstream of a single base, after excluding nodes where one popular derivative carries most of the exposure.

A node with three descendants and 97.5% of their downloads in one is not a dependency; it is one model with siblings. 36 of 170 nodes are excluded on that basis. "Inherits" means "is downstream of" — an upper bound on exposure, not a prediction that a flaw propagates.
